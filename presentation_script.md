## 🎤 2. Presentation Step-by-Step Flow

### **Step 1: The Citizen Experience (Start Here)**
1. **Log in as Citizen** (`amitjha@example.com`).
2. **Action:** Navigate to **"Report Emergency"**. Fill out the form to report a new crisis (e.g., "Severe Flooding in Downtown").
3. **Show AI:** Click the **AI Copilot** (bottom right sparkle icon). Ask: *"What should I do during a flood?"* to demonstrate how AI provides immediate, life-saving advice to citizens.
4. **Log out.**

### **Step 2: Administrator Dashboard & AI Triage (The Core Demo)**
1. **Log in as Administrator** (`admin@example.com`).
2. **Dashboard Overview:** 
   - Point out the metrics. 
   - **Show AI:** Highlight the **AI Insight Panel** at the top. Explain how the AI instantly analyzes all platform data to give a real-time situation summary, saving the admin from manually reviewing raw data.
3. **Emergencies Page:**
   - Navigate to **"Emergencies"**. You will see the emergency just reported by the citizen.
   - Click the **"Verify"** button to approve it.
   - **Show AI:** Click **"AI Prioritize"**. Explain how the AI automatically sorts emergencies by severity, ensuring the admin focuses on the most critical situations first.

### **Step 3: AI-Driven Volunteer Matching**
1. **Navigate to "Assignments".**
2. **The Problem:** Explain that normally, an admin has to manually read through volunteer profiles to find the right people for the job.
3. **Show AI Solution:** Click the **"AI Match"** button next to the emergency. 
   - The AI automatically pairs the best volunteers based on their skills and location. 
   - Assign a volunteer to the emergency.

### **Step 4: AI Resource Recommendation**
1. **Navigate to "Resources".**
2. **The Problem:** Explain that knowing exactly what supplies to send to different disasters takes time and research.
3. **Show AI Solution:** Select the active emergency and click the **"AI Suggest"** button.
   - The AI will instantly analyze the *disaster type* and *location*, and output a smart, tailored list of recommended resources (e.g., Sandbags and Life Jackets for a flood).
   - Show how the admin can now immediately click **"Add Resource"** and input the AI's exact recommendations.

### **Step 5: The Omnipresent AI Copilot**
1. While still in the Admin panel, open the **AI Response Copilot** (bottom right).
2. Click on a suggested prompt like **"Available Volunteers"** or **"Situation Overview"**. 
3. Explain that the AI acts as a 24/7 assistant, dynamically aware of the current database state, ready to answer any specific admin query on the fly.

### **Step 6: Closing the Loop (Volunteer & Donor)**
1. **Log out & Log in as Volunteer** (`volunteer@example.com`).
   - Navigate to **"My Assignments"**. Show the assignment created by the Admin.
   - Show the **AI Copilot** giving the volunteer a personalized briefing about their task.
2. **Log out & Log in as Donor** (`donor@example.com`).
   - Navigate to **"Verified Emergencies"**. Show how the donor can see the verified crisis.
   - Navigate to **"My Pledges"** and pledge a donation (e.g., funds or physical supplies).

When concluding your presentation, emphasize these points:
* *"By integrating AI across every module, we transitioned the platform from a simple database to an **active decision-support system**."*
* *"The Administrator is no longer overwhelmed by data. AI handles the heavy lifting: it **prioritizes crises**, **matches personnel**, and **predicts resource needs**, cutting response times drastically."*
* *"We didn't just add a chatbot; we embedded **Role-Aware AI**. The Copilot knows if it's talking to a terrified citizen, an active volunteer, or the system administrator, and adapts its help accordingly."*
